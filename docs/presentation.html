Capstone submission · 4 November 2026

HomeServe

Home services, booked properly. A customer picks a verified crew in Ahmedabad, a provider assigns a technician, and the job is paid and reviewed.

Four roles · ten modules · Swagger · live URL

Problem

A visit should have one record.

For the customer

Search, a free hour, a price with GST, and a person who actually arrives.

For the crew

Accept the job, send the right technician, and see what they earned after commission.

For the platform

Verify the provider, stop a double booking, and export the numbers.

What we built

One app for all four roles, with the API documented in Swagger.

Quality

Tests and security

Tests

9 pytest cases passed on 4 Nov 2026. They cover coupon math, the full book-assign-pay-review path, role denial, password reset, and the admin CSV.

Security

21 controls in docs/SECURITY.md. Hashed passwords, expiring tokens, rate limits, upload limits, and no paid status without the success card or a Razorpay signature.
